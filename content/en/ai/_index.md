**Preview.** This section starts to catalogue the AI accelerators, NPU IP, and AI SoCs in the RISC-V ecosystem, and to define the fields and scoring used for the upcoming "model / operator / software-version maturity" matrix.

Current data covers only publicly documented hardware specifications and toolchain information; items without a public benchmark are not given invented scores. A few **ARM-based AI SoCs** are kept for reference and are explicitly marked in the "architecture" field and in the filters. Measured results and corrections are welcome via issues.
